Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026
Publisher: Drixel Labs Incorporation (Drixel Labs Inc.)


1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to A-Chatz. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data processed through our application and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights.

By creating an account or using A-Chatz, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.


2. DATA CONTROLLER

The data controller for A-Chatz is Drixel Labs Incorporation (Drixel Labs Inc.). For privacy enquiries contact: support@a-chatz.com.


3. DATA WE COLLECT

We may collect and process:

  • Identity Data: display name, username, profile photo, account type.
  • Contact Data: email address, phone number (if provided), linked account identifiers.
  • Content Data: messages, media, status updates, voice notes, and metadata you choose to send.
  • Technical Data: device type, app version, IP address, crash logs, and authentication tokens.
  • Usage Data: feature interactions, notification preferences, theme and settings choices.
  • Location Data: only when you explicitly share live location in a chat.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use personal data to:

  • Provide and maintain messaging, calls, status, channels, and AI features.
  • Authenticate users and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Deliver push notifications you have enabled.
  • Improve security, performance, and product quality.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

Legal bases may include contract performance, legitimate interests, consent (where required), and legal duty.


5. SHARING & THIRD PARTIES

We use trusted infrastructure providers (including Firebase/Google Cloud) to host authentication, databases, storage, and notifications. These providers process data under their own terms and our instructions. We do not sell your personal data.

Messages are stored to deliver the service. End-to-end encryption features, where enabled, limit what we can read—but you should still exercise caution with sensitive content.


6. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Data may be processed in countries other than your own. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards where required by law.


7. RETENTION

We retain data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backup, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. You may request deletion subject to applicable law.


8. SECURITY

We implement administrative, technical, and organisational measures designed to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; use A-Chatz at your own risk for highly sensitive information.


9. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, or port your data, and to withdraw consent. Contact support@a-chatz.com to exercise these rights.


10. CHILDREN

A-Chatz is not intended for users under 16 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.


11. CHANGES

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app with an updated date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.


12. CONTACT
