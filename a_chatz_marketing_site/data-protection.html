Data Protection & Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: May 2026
Publisher: Drixel Labs Incorporation (Drixel Labs Inc.)


1. PURPOSE

This policy defines permitted and prohibited uses of A-Chatz to keep the platform safe and lawful while protecting data integrity.


2. PERMITTED USE

  • Private and group messaging with consenting participants.
  • Sharing status updates, media, and voice notes you have rights to distribute.
  • Business accounts following applicable marketing and consumer laws.
  • Legitimate use of AI assistant features for productivity.

3. PROHIBITED USE

You may not:

  • Send unsolicited bulk messages (spam) or phishing links.
  • Share child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or exploit minors — zero tolerance; reports to authorities.
  • Promote violence, terrorism, or self-harm instructions.
  • Distribute malware, credential stealers, or surveillance tools.
  • Circumvent bans, blocks, or security controls.
  • Harvest user data without consent.
  • Use the Service for illegal gambling, drugs, or weapons sales where prohibited.

4. ENFORCEMENT

Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, permanent ban, and referral to law enforcement. We cooperate with valid legal requests.


5. REPORTING

Report abuse via in-app tools or support@a-chatz.com with relevant chat IDs and screenshots where safe to do so.


Community Guidelines


Our community thrives on respect and privacy. Please:

  • Treat others with dignity — no hate speech based on race, gender, religion, disability, or orientation.
  • Obtain consent before sharing someone else's photos, videos, or private information.
  • Use view-once and locked chats thoughtfully for sensitive content.
  • Respect "Do Not Disturb" and muted status preferences.
  • Verify identities before sending money or personal documents.

Do not:

  • No bullying, stalking, or coordinated harassment.
  • No fake emergency alerts or abusive SOS triggers.
  • No impersonation of Drixel Labs Inc. staff or officials.

Business accounts must clearly identify themselves and honour opt-out requests.

We may update these guidelines. Founders Anelisa Thelejane and Andzani Mashabane thank you for helping build a premium, trustworthy community.
