Ironclad Security & Privacy

Your dialogues are strictly your intellectual property. At Drixel Labs Inc., we architected A-Chatz around an uncompromising privacy-first paradigm.

Advanced Local Storage Encryption

Unlike ordinary messaging applications that store your chats in plain text on their servers, A-Chatz utilizes a revolutionary local storage encryption model. Your messages are downloaded and written into the local memory of your specific device using an impenetrable `cipherText` payload. This means:

  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture: We cannot read your locally encrypted SQLite/Hive databases.
  • Device Theft Protection: Even if your device is physically compromised or stolen, malicious actors cannot extract the raw database files and read your chats, as the local storage is strictly bound to your Biometric hardware keys.
  • Decrypted Text Renders: The `decrypted_text.dart` module only reconstructs the human-readable message inside the volatile RAM while you are looking at the screen, immediately destroying the keys when you close the app.

Granular Privacy

Take absolute control over your digital footprint. Our Firestore privacy nodes allow you to customize who can view your Profile, Last Seen, and Statuses. Lock specific highly-sensitive chat rooms behind secondary PINs or Face ID using our Locked Chats feature.
