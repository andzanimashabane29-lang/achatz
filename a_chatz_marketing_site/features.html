Unprecedented Capabilities

Meticulously crafted functionalities bridging the gap between sophisticated engineering and intuitive user experience. Explore the massive suite of A-Chatz features.

Advanced Chat Ecosystem

Decrypted Text

Experience true end-to-end encryption. Your messages are stored locally on your device as encrypted cipher text, ensuring absolute privacy and uncompromising security that cannot be breached remotely.

Archived & Locked Chats

Keep your main chat list incredibly clean by archiving inactive threads. For highly sensitive conversations, securely lock them behind native device biometric authentication (Face ID or Fingerprint).

Starred Messages & Quick Replies

Pin important messages for immediate access later, or configure quick reply templates to send instantaneous, pre-written responses when you are in a rush.

In-App Tipping System

A fully integrated, seamless tipping system that allows you to send digital tokens or real currency directly within the chat window, perfect for supporting creators or reimbursing friends.

Call & Live Broadcasting

Interactive Live Streams

Transcend traditional calls. Go live to your followers with an interactive host interface, or join a broadcast as a viewer with floating reactions and real-time live comments.

Floating Call Overlay (PiP)

Never let a phone call interrupt your workflow again. Active calls instantly transition into a floating Picture-in-Picture (PiP) overlay, allowing you to freely navigate the entire app while talking.

Next-Gen Chat & Safety

Smart Document Previews

Never guess what's inside a file again. A-Chatz automatically generates beautiful cover previews for your PDFs, Word documents, Excel sheets, and PowerPoint presentations directly inside the chat bubble.

Active Safety Overlay

Your safety is paramount. Share your Live Map Location securely, configure Emergency Contacts, and utilize our Accident Prevention tools to ensure you and your loved ones are always protected on the move.

Locked & Archived Chats

Maintain absolute privacy with secure Locked Chats hidden behind biometric authentication, and keep your inbox perfectly organized with our intuitive Archive system.

Immersive Media & AI

On-Demand AI Actions

Highlight any message to access intelligent AI Actions. Instantly translate foreign languages, have our Professional AI rewrite your drafts into eloquent paragraphs, or use AI Smart Replies to respond effortlessly.

Advanced Live Streaming

Host high-definition PiP (Picture-in-Picture) WebRTC calls with built-in Call Effects and Floating Reactions, bringing unparalleled interactivity to your video communications.

Generative AI Stickers

Why search for stickers when you can make them? Simply type a prompt and instantly generate custom, expressive, and unique stickers using our built-in generative AI engine right inside your chat.

Channels & Communities

Advanced Owner Analytics

Creators rejoice! Gain deep insights into your Channel's performance, track engagement metrics, and discover exactly how your audience is interacting with your broadcasts.

Extended 4-Minute Statuses

Break free from the 30-second limitation. Share rich, compelling stories and updates with your network using our expanded 4-minute Status video capabilities.

A-Chatz For Business

Pro Business Verification

Designed for growing brands. Obtain official Verified Badges, configure automated Business Hours and Quick Replies, and list unlimited products in the global A-Chatz marketplace catalog.

Tipping & Invoice System

Monetize your interactions directly. Our built-in payment integration allows seamless tipping in chats and the generation of professional invoices for your clients without leaving the app.
