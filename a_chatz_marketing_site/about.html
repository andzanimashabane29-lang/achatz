The Drixel Labs Vision

Pioneering the future of secure, untethered human connectivity.

A Parent Company of Innovation

A-Chatz is the flagship communication application proudly developed and published by Drixel Labs Incorporation (Drixel Labs Inc.). It is important to understand that Drixel Labs is not merely the creator of a single application; it is a sprawling, forward-thinking parent company that conceptualizes, produces, and operates a multitude of software companies, digital solutions, and high-end technological platforms across the globe.

Founded in 2026

Drixel Labs and the A-Chatz ecosystem were officially founded in 2026. In a world where digital privacy was becoming increasingly compromised by legacy platforms, Drixel Labs was born to set a new, uncompromising standard. Within its first year, it introduced revolutionary WebRTC latency protocols, integrated AI capabilities natively into chat systems, and redefined the luxury digital experience.

Our Visionary Founders

Drixel Labs was established with a singular, unwavering mission by two technological visionaries:

  • Anelisa Thelejane (Co-Founder): Anelisa's deep expertise in scalable architecture and user-centric design paradigms laid the foundation for A-Chatz's stunning, glassmorphic visual identity and its lightning-fast Flutter codebase.
  • Andzani Mashabane (Co-Founder): Andzani spearheaded the integration of impenetrable security protocols, the proprietary AI engines (such as the AI Note Taker), and the robust WebRTC low-latency streaming infrastructure that powers our Host/Viewer live broadcasting system.

Under the meticulous guidance of Anelisa and Andzani, the dedicated engineering team at Drixel Labs Inc. constructs platforms that are not only blazingly fast and aesthetically luxurious but are fortified with impregnable security protocols to ensure your data remains exclusively in your hands.
