Download for Android

Follow these simple steps to install the official A-Chatz APK directly on your device:

  1. Tap the green download button below to fetch the a-chatz.apk file.
  2. Open your device's Downloads folder (or swipe down your notification panel) and tap the downloaded file.
  3. If prompted, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source".
  4. Tap Install and then open the app!

Install on iPhone / iPad

A-Chatz leverages advanced PWA technology to run natively on your iOS device without the App Store.

  1. Open the Web Portal using Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner. A-Chatz is now installed on your home screen!

Download for Windows Desktop

Experience the full power of A-Chatz right from your computer desktop.

  1. Click the download button below to grab the A-Chatz Desktop .zip package.
  2. Once downloaded, right-click the zip file and select "Extract All".
  3. Open the extracted folder and double-click a-chatz.exe to launch the application.
