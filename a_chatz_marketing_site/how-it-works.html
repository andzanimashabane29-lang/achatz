How A-Chatz Functions

A sophisticated yet accessible onboarding trajectory.

01

Effortless Registration & Login

Our infrastructure utilizes Firebase Authentication to ensure impenetrable account security while making login seamless. To get started:

  1. Open the A-Chatz app and tap "Get Started".
  2. Enter your cellular Phone Number or an Email address.
  3. We will instantly dispatch a secure One-Time Password (OTP) to your device.
  4. Enter the OTP to verify your identity.

Once verified, you can effortlessly set up biometric authentication (Face ID or Fingerprint) in your device settings for instantaneous future logins without needing a password.

Biometric Security

Authenticate effortlessly.

02

Initiating Conversations & Live Streams

Navigate our luxuriously dark, glassmorphic interface to locate the 'Chats' directory. Selecting a contact triggers our Cloud Functions to instantiate a secure private channel. Furthermore, transitioning to the 'Calls' tab allows you to effortlessly toggle between becoming a broadcasting 'Host' or an engaged 'Viewer' in high-definition live streams.

03

Customizing Privacy & Profile

Within the 'Settings' nomenclature, you possess absolute sovereignty over your digital footprint. Configure your 'Last Seen', 'Profile Photo', and 'Status' visibility (Everyone, Contacts, or Nobody) through our Firestore privacy nodes. This granular control ensures your interactions remain precisely as public or clandestine as you desire.
